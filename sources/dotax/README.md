# Hawaiʻi DOTAX tax-credit claims, tax year 2023

**What this is.** Hawaiʻi Department of Taxation, *Tax Credits Claimed
by Hawaiʻi Taxpayers — Tax Year 2023* (73 pp). Retrieved 2026-09-06
from https://files.hawaii.gov/tax/stats/stats/credits/2023credit.pdf

**File.** `HI_DOTAX_Tax_Credits_Claimed_TY2023.pdf`, sha256
`5bc3afb80e60428e5e31857640dde29405173c15e288ba991e96aeaa8eef70bb`.

**Load-bearing sentence (verbatim).** "The second largest tax credit
was the renewable energy technologies income tax credit, which
amounted to $100.1 million, or 16.1% of the total."

**What it supports.** Report §2.3's international-rooftop paragraph:
if streamlined rules brought installed costs near Australian or German
levels, the state's renewable-energy technologies income tax credit
(RETITC, HRS §235-12.5) — roughly $100 million per year at 2023 claim
levels — would not be needed.

**Caveats.** RETITC covers solar PV, solar thermal, and wind (PV
dominates claims); the $100.1M is claims against tax year 2023,
including carryovers from earlier installations. Claims fluctuate:
2022 was lower (the report notes a $10.3M year-over-year decrease in
one taxpayer category), so "roughly $100 million per year" describes
the most recent year, not a stable average.
