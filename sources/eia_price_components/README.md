Shares: generation 58.4%, transmission 13.2%, distribution 28.3%.
(The nominal-dollar block in the same table gives identical shares.)

Used in Box 4.2 as the national benchmark against which the IGP base
plan's transmission share of capital (63%, Tables 9-2/9-4 of the May 2023
IGP report) is set. Note the comparison is imperfect in a conservative
direction: the EIA shares are of the DELIVERED PRICE (capital plus fuel
plus O&M), while the 63% is a share of CAPITAL EXPENDITURE only. Fuel is
most of generation's price share and is absent from a capex program, so
transmission's share of ordinary utility CAPEX runs higher than 13% —
LBNL's cost-trends work puts distribution alone at ~44% of IOU capex.
The status-quo scenario's own 12% capex share is therefore the cleaner
benchmark, and it is internal to the same IGP tables.
