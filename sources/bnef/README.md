# BloombergNEF 2025 battery price survey (press release)

**What this is.** BloombergNEF's public press release for its *2025
Lithium-Ion Battery Price Survey* (December 9, 2025; lead author Evelina
Stoikou, head of BNEF's battery technology team). Retrieved 2026-09-06
from
https://about.bnef.com/insights/clean-transport/lithium-ion-battery-pack-prices-fall-to-108-per-kilowatt-hour-despite-rising-metal-prices-bloombergnef/
The full survey is available to BNEF clients only; the press release
carries the numbers cited here.

**Load-bearing sentences (verbatim).**
- "Battery pack prices for stationary storage dropped to $70/kWh in
  2025, 45% lower than in 2024. This is the sharpest drop across all
  segments, making stationary storage the lowest-priced segment for the
  first time."
- Headline: global volume-weighted average pack price fell 8% to
  $108/kWh across all segments in 2025.

**What it supports.** The 45 percent end of the report §2 box sentence
"battery costs … have fallen 30–45 percent" (the 30–38 percent end is
IRENA's *Renewable Power Generation Costs in 2024*, ch. 9 —
`sources/IRENA_RPGC_in_2024_ch9_slice.pdf`).

**Caveats.** BNEF's figure is a **pack price** (cells + pack hardware),
volume-weighted across global buyers; it excludes inverters, balance of
system, installation, and developer costs, so it is an upper bound on
how fast a completed Hawaiʻi installation's cost falls. IRENA's
companion figure (32–38 percent, 2024 vs 2023) is a **total installed
cost** for utility-scale BESS. The two measure different scopes and
different year pairs (2024→2025 vs 2023→2024); the report cites them as
a range, "30–45 percent."
