# Hawaiian Electric competitive-bidding RFPs and model PPA

Vendored to support the Section 2.7 procurement-reform discussion: the
evolution of PPA price terms across procurement rounds.

- `Stage2_Oahu_RFP_2019-08-22.pdf` — Final Stage 2 Oʻahu Variable RDG RFP.
  Section 3.9.2: "Escalation in pricing over the term of the RDG PPA or the
  term of the ESPPA is prohibited." No inflation-adjustment mechanism of any
  kind (0 hits for GDPIPD / inflation / price adjustment across 207 pages).
  URL: hawaiianelectric.com/documents/clean_energy_hawaii/selling_power_to_the_utility/competitive_bidding/20190822_final_stage_2_oahu_variable_rfp.pdf
- `Stage3_Hawaii_RFP_2022-11-07.pdf` — Proposed Final Stage 3 Hawaiʻi RFP.
  Section 4 (BAFO): introduces "a one-time pricing adjustment of their
  BAFO-defined Lump Sum Payment amounts … capped to be no greater than a ten
  percent (10%) adjustment. If there is no inflation during the time period or
  the index decreases, pricing will remain as bid in the BAFO."
  URL: .../20221107_exhibit_4_s3_hawaii_RFP.pdf
- `IGP_RFP_AppxJ_Model_RDG_PPA.pdf` — current Model RDG PPA (IGP RFP,
  Appendix J). Attachment J Section 2(d) "BAFO Unit Price Adjustment":
  GDPIPD-indexed one-time adjustment, BAFO submission to PUC Approval Order
  Date, 10% cap; Section 2(f) Tariff Rate Adjustment; combined 15% cap.
  Unit Price otherwise fixed nominal for the contract term.
  URL: .../igp_rfp/igp_rfp_appx_j_rdg_ppa.pdf
